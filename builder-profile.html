About Lodha Group

Lodha Group is India's largest luxury real estate developer by sales, delivering excellence since 1980. From iconic skyscrapers in Mumbai to luxury townships in London, Lodha's properties redefine the concept of premium living. With over 85,000 homes delivered and 350+ projects, Lodha is synonymous with quality, trust, and luxury.

Their portfolio includes Palava City (India's most livable city), World Towers (The World's Tallest Residential Towers), Trump Tower Mumbai, and over 40 luxury branded residences.
